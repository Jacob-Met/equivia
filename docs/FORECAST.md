EQUIVIA distinguishes the chance of the other lamp being on in one trial from its possible total across a three-trial click. The result view compares your committed estimate with the candidates' prediction before that click and their revised prediction immediately afterward.

For three trials, the view also shows the probabilities of seeing the other lamp on zero, one, two, or three times. The hidden world stays fixed throughout the click. EQUIVIA calculates the count distribution within each candidate, then combines these distributions using the pre-run candidate weights. In the initial matching-lamps case, holding A on gives a 3/4 chance that B is on in one trial, but a 9/16 chance that B is on in all three; cubing 3/4 would give the wrong count prediction.
