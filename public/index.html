What would happen if you intervened?

“A is on” and “I held A on” may look identical. They can mean very different things.

Seeing A on, or holding it on?

Seeing keeps only natural results with that lamp state. Holding replaces the lamp’s rule and lets the other lamp respond.

What is this lab assuming?

These are authored binary worlds. A root lamp is a fair random bit; the other follows its parent. The hidden structural world stays fixed, while every trial draws fresh noise. A clamp sets one variable without changing the other's rule.

Exact enumeration establishes equality for these models. The observations you collect are finite random samples. This laboratory does not establish causes in the outside world.
