EQUIVIA is a practice lab for cause and effect. It helps students and curious people learn how to test an idea. Two lamps, A and B, always agree. Your job is to find out which lamp drives the other. Holding a lamp is different from watching it. Here you hold lamp A off, and see what lamp B does. Before each run, write down your guess. Type forty percent for how often lamp B will be on. Now run one trial. The lab records what happened and keeps the record. The balance on the left shows how well each explanation fits the results so far. It tips as evidence comes in. Below the lamps, the lab compares your guess with the models before and after the run. Run three more trials. A short run can look different from a single guess, and that is normal. Open the note for the first trial. It shows how that one result changed the weights. The lab keeps your notebook on this device. Save it as a file to reopen it later, or share an evidence report. Make a guess, test it, and let the evidence decide how far it goes.
