# EQUIVIA demo script (draft, not rendered)

Source: `demo/demo.json`. Dry-run verified: every selector existed when its step ran, no console errors, step screenshots in `demo/steps/`. About 79 seconds of narration at 150 words per minute, 11 steps. Lamp outcomes are random, so on-screen numbers differ between runs; the narration only claims what is true in every run.

| Step | Narration | What is on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | EQUIVIA is a practice lab for cause and effect. It helps students and curious people learn how to test an idea. | Start page: the two lamps, balance at fifty-fifty, "1 · Predict" panel, notebook empty. |
| 2 | Two lamps, A and B, always agree. Your job is to find out which lamp drives the other. | Same screen, scrolled to the top. |
| 3 | Holding a lamp is different from watching it. Here you hold lamp A off, and see what lamp B does. | Lamp A switched to Off under "Held by your clamp". |
| 4 | Before each run, write down your guess. Type forty percent for how often lamp B will be on. | Probability field shows 40 with the accent edge. Run buttons enabled. |
| 5 | Now run one trial. The lab records what happened and keeps the record. | Trial count drops to five of six. Notebook shows entry 01. |
| 6 | The balance on the left shows how well each explanation fits the results so far. It tips as evidence comes in. | Balance tilted toward one explanation, with its weight shown. |
| 7 | Below the lamps, the lab compares your guess with the models before and after the run. | "Reflect on this run" panel with your estimate, the models before, and the updated value. |
| 8 | Run three more trials. A short run can look different from a single guess, and that is normal. | Two trials left. Notebook has four entries; count table shows the observed count. |
| 9 | Open the note for the first trial. It shows how that one result changed the weights. | First entry's "How trial 1 changed the weights" open, showing before, chance and after. |
| 10 | The lab keeps your notebook on this device. Save it as a file to reopen it later, or share an evidence report. | Page scrolled back to the top, showing Save notebook and the Coach and studio drawer. |
| 11 | Make a guess, test it, and let the evidence decide how far it goes. | Top of the page; closing frame. |

Not in the demo: the coach and world-authoring features (they need a model key), and the reveal, since finishing the investigation ends it.
